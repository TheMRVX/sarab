# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We aim to make participation in Sarab welcoming, respectful and free of harassment for everyone, regardless of experience, identity or background.

## Expected behavior

- Be considerate and constructive; critique technical decisions rather than people.
- Assume good intent while addressing concrete issues.
- Respect different levels of experience and communicate with care.
- Credit others' work, respect licenses and avoid publishing sensitive information.
- Accept moderation decisions made to protect project participants.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, threats, hate speech, discriminatory insults, sexualized harassment, doxxing, intimidation, repeated unwanted contact and deliberate disruption are not tolerated. Posting credentials or someone else's private data is prohibited.

## Scope and enforcement

These expectations apply to GitHub issues, pull requests, discussions and other Sarab community spaces. Maintainers may edit or remove inappropriate content, warn participants, restrict participation or ban repeat offenders, considering impact and context.

For conduct concerns, contact the maintainer privately using contact information made available on their [GitHub profile](https://github.com/TheMRVX); avoid posting sensitive details publicly. A dedicated confidential conduct-reporting address has not been published. In cases of immediate danger, contact appropriate local authorities or platform safety channels.

This project-specific policy is inspired by the [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/) and [GitHub community guidelines](https://docs.github.com/en/communities).
